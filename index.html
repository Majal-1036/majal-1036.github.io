I am a Research Engineer at Zunitech LLC with an M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Wisconsin–Madison and a B.S. in Computer Engineering from the University of Engineering and Technology, Lahore. My research lies at the intersection of probabilistic machine learning, robust Bayesian estimation, and uncertainty-aware learning. I develop reliable estimation and learning methods for systems affected by outliers, noisy measurements, and imperfect data, with applications in robotics, perception, localization, and sensor fusion. My graduate research also explored extreme-value-theory-based error bounds for neural-network perception in robust vision-based control.
