Selected research

Reliable learning and estimation under imperfect data

My work develops probabilistic and learning-based methods that remain useful when measurements are biased, correlated, heteroscedastic, or contaminated by outliers.

Adaptive robust filtering

EMORF-II: Adaptive EM-Based Outlier-Robust Filtering

EMORF-II addresses nonlinear state estimation when measurement noise is correlated and observations contain outliers. The method combines Bayesian inference with Expectation-Maximization to estimate the system state, detect outliers, and adapt their characteristics during inference, reducing reliance on manually chosen outlier parameters.

Contribution: Led the development, implementation, simulation design, and evaluation of the method across changing outlier probabilities and sensor configurations.

Trajectory reconstruction

Outlier-Robust Unscented RTS Smoothing

This work introduces an Unscented Rauch–Tung–Striebel smoother that selectively suppresses corrupted measurement dimensions instead of discarding an entire observation vector. The approach preserves useful sensor information and improves offline trajectory reconstruction in multi-sensor systems.

Contribution: Developed the algorithm and MATLAB implementation, designed the experiments, and benchmarked its accuracy and computational scaling against contemporary robust smoothers.

Bias-aware estimation

Variational Bayesian Filtering with Biased Observations

The Bias Detecting and Mitigating filter embeds bias detection directly within nonlinear Bayesian filtering. It models the unknown presence and magnitude of measurement bias, then uses variational inference to compensate for both temporary and persistent bias without depending on a separate external detector.

Contribution: Contributed to algorithm development, MATLAB implementation, numerical evaluation, and comparison with nonlinear filtering baselines and theoretical error bounds.

Reliable GNSS

Dictionary-Based Contrastive Learning for GNSS Jamming Detection

This work develops a compact contrastive-learning pipeline for detecting GNSS interference from spectrogram representations of raw IQ data. Dictionary-based representation learning, structured pruning, and knowledge distillation improve class separation in limited-data settings while reducing memory and inference demands for embedded deployment.

Contribution: Co-developed the learning framework and contributed to model compression, experiments, benchmarking, and manuscript preparation.

M.S. thesis

Extreme-Value-Theory Error Bounds for Vision-Based Control

My master’s research used Extreme Value Theory to estimate local norm error bounds for learning-based and model-based perception algorithms. The work focuses on statistically characterizing perception errors near nominal trajectories so that vision-based control systems can reason more explicitly about uncertainty and safety.

Contribution: Developed the EVT-based methodology, created simulation and evaluation pipelines, analyzed perception-error growth, and documented the work in my M.S. thesis.
